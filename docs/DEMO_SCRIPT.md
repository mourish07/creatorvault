# CreatorVault Demo Script

**Estimated Time:** 2–3 minutes

## Preparation

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Load the `dist` folder as an unpacked extension in `chrome://extensions`.
3. Open a new tab and navigate to Instagram (`https://www.instagram.com/`).
4. Ensure you are logged into an Instagram account (or can access public profiles).
5. Pin the CreatorVault extension icon to your Chrome toolbar for easy access.

---

## The Demo

### 1. Introduction & The Problem
*(Open the Instagram homepage)*

"Hi, I'm going to demonstrate **CreatorVault**, a seamless CRM designed specifically for influencer marketers. The core problem marketers face is constantly jumping between Instagram and a messy spreadsheet to track potential creators. CreatorVault solves this by bringing the CRM directly to the creator's profile."

### 2. Profile Detection & Capture
*(Navigate to a creator profile, e.g., `@natgeo` or a popular influencer)*

"When I land on a creator's profile, CreatorVault instantly recognizes it. Notice this polished 'Add to CRM' floating button that appears in the bottom right corner. It feels like a native part of the experience."

*(Click the 'Add to CRM' button)*

"Clicking it opens our capture panel. Behind the scenes, the extension has already safely extracted the creator's public information—username, display name, follower count, bio, and verified status. No manual data entry required."

### 3. Adding Context
*(Type in the Tags and Notes fields)*

"But a CRM needs context. I can quickly add a few tags—let's select 'Photography' and add a custom tag 'Q3 Campaign'. I'll also add a quick note: *'Great reach, potential fit for the upcoming launch.'*"

*(Change the Pipeline Status dropdown)*

"I'll set the pipeline status to 'Contacted', and hit **Save to CRM**."

*(Wait for the success animation)*

"We get a clean success confirmation, and the floating button now indicates this creator is safely in our vault."

### 4. Duplicate Prevention
*(Refresh the page or click the floating button again)*

"If another team member or I forget and try to add this creator again, CreatorVault catches it. It recognizes the normalized username and switches the panel into 'Update' mode. This prevents messy duplicate records in our database."

### 5. The CRM Dashboard
*(Click the CreatorVault icon in the Chrome toolbar, then click 'Open CRM')*

"Now let's look at the actual CRM. This dashboard is a full React application running entirely locally within the extension. It's fast, private, and secure."

*(Point out the metrics and table)*

"At the top, we have actionable metrics tracking our pipeline. Below is our creator database. You can see the creator we just added, complete with their formatted follower count, status badge, and tags."

### 6. Search, Filter, and Export
*(Type a letter into the search bar, then use the Status dropdown)*

"As the database grows, finding creators is instant. The search filters across names, bios, and tags in real-time. I can easily filter to see only creators I'm currently 'Collaborating' with."

*(Click the 'Export CSV' button in the top right)*

"And because data shouldn't be locked away, I can click 'Export CSV' to instantly download a clean, formatted spreadsheet of my entire database to share with the rest of the marketing team."

### 7. Conclusion

"CreatorVault takes a fragmented workflow and turns it into a smooth, professional process—all without leaving the browser. Thank you!"
